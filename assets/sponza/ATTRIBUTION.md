# Sponza model attribution

The Atrium Sponza model was created by Frank Meinl of Crytek, based on the
original Sponza model by Marko Dabrovic. Morgan McGuire modified and prepared
the OBJ export and textures.

Source: [McGuire Computer Graphics Archive, Sponza model](https://casual-effects.com/g3d/data10/index.html)

The archive identifies this model as available under the [Creative Commons
Attribution 3.0 license (CC BY 3.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The original `copyright.txt` from the archive is included alongside these assets.
